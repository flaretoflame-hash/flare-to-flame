# Flare to Flame — Marketing Skill

All marketing output must reflect the FTF brand: luxury, warm, aspirational, cinematic. English only.

---

## BRAND VOICE

- **Tone:** Confident, warm, aspirational — like a trusted luxury expert speaking to a friend
- **Never:** Clinical, pushy, salesy, discounting language, or price mentions
- **Always:** Outcome-focused, transformation-based, emotionally resonant
- **Language:** English only — no Hinglish, no Hindi

---

## CONTENT PILLARS (FTF)

Build all content around these 5 themes:

1. **Transformation** — Before/after of skin, hair, nails. Emotional outcomes. "You walked in tired. You walked out radiant."
2. **Expertise** — Product knowledge (Olaplex, Rica, O3+, Moroccanoil, Wella, Kanpeki, De Fabulous, Florantic Professional, Organica Da Roma). Techniques. Education.
3. **Ritual** — The salon experience as a self-care ritual, not a transaction. Sensory details. Atmosphere.
4. **Trust** — Behind the scenes. Team. Process. Safety and hygiene.
5. **Invitation** — Soft CTAs to WhatsApp. Never hard selling.

---

## INSTAGRAM POST FRAMEWORK

**Structure for every post:**

1. **Hook (line 1):** Bold statement or question. No "Hi" or "Welcome." Start with emotion or outcome.
2. **Body (2-4 lines):** Expand on the hook. Sensory, specific, benefit-led.
3. **CTA (last line):** Soft invite to WhatsApp. Example: "Ready to begin? Chat with us on WhatsApp."
4. **No prices** anywhere in caption.
5. **No salon address** until opening day.
6. **3-5 lines max** for captions. Short and powerful.

**Hashtag approach (3-7 max):**
#LuxurySalon #FlareToFlame #HairCare #SkinCare #SelfCare #GlowUp #SalonLife

---

## WHATSAPP MESSAGE FRAMEWORK

- Max 3 lines
- Conversational, warm — like a human, not a broadcast
- No prices
- Always end with a question to invite reply
- Example: "Hi [Name]! Your last visit was [X] days ago. Ready for a refresh? Let us know when works for you 🌟"

## VIDEO SCRIPT FRAMEWORK (Aisha / Flare Engine)

4-scene cinematic structure — 40 seconds total:

| Scene | Name | Content |
|-------|------|---------|
| 1 | Hook | Bold opening question or statement — 5-8 seconds |
| 2 | Vulnerability | The problem your client feels — 8-10 seconds |
| 3 | Transformation | What FTF does differently — 12-15 seconds |
| 4 | CTA | Soft invite — never hard sell — 5-8 seconds |

- Language: English only
- Tone: Warm, cinematic, expert
- Never mention prices
- Never mention address

## HARD STOPS

1. No prices anywhere — not in posts, captions, messages, scripts
2. No salon address or location until opening day
3. No Hinglish — English only
4. No hard selling — always soft invite
5. No dark backgrounds on visual content
6. No more than 5 cards per page
7. Tone must always be warm and aspirational — never clinical
